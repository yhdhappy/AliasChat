PrivyAI is on

Masks personal data in your messages before they reach ChatGPT or Claude. The AI only sees placeholders; you see the real values.

How your data is handled

  • All masking happens locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • The placeholder map is kept in the browser's session storage — in-memory only, cleared when you close the browser. Web pages cannot access it.
  • Optional settings you save are synced via your own browser account (chrome.storage.sync).
  • PrivyAI does not collect analytics, telemetry, or any data about you.

Limitations

  • Detection is pattern-based. Names without context, or data in formats we don't recognize, may not be masked.
  • PDF and image uploads are blocked by default (they are not inspected in the browser).
